MoE expert cache measured live: +38%/+52% decode -> 2 new presets

Downloaded upstream b11541 (first nightly with --moe-cache-mib, PR #29887)
into llama.cpp/versions/b11541-cuda-13.4/ and benchmarked the cache ladder
on both MoE models (96K, q4_0 KV, port-8099 protocol):

- Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q6_K, -cmoe --moe-cache-mib 11500: 40.8 t/s steady
  warm-cache decode (42.1/39.5) vs 29.6 beellama record = +38%; pp 422->251.
- Gemma-4-26B-A4B Q4_K_M, -cmoe --moe-cache-mib 10500: 72.2 t/s steady
  (74.7/69.7) vs 47.5 = +52%; pp 1428->1101.
- Cache scales with size to the VRAM edge; LRU cold-start is real (first
  ~200 tokens 25-47 t/s below steady; warm up before measuring).
- Measured negative: --fit + --moe-cache-mib oversubscribes 16 GB (free
  ~200 MB -> WDDM spill -> 3.65 t/s). Do not combine; place experts
  explicitly and size the cache by hand.
